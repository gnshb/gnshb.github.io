---
title: Notes on Building Small Research Tools
date: 2026-02-18
summary: Three patterns that make throwaway scripts more useful over time.
---

Small tools are worth building when they remove repeated friction, not when they try to become mini-products.

Three habits help a lot:

- Keep inputs and outputs obvious
- Prefer text files and simple formats
- Write down the command that produced each result

Most research tooling does not need a polished interface. It needs to be easy to rerun, easy to inspect, and easy to delete when it stops being useful.

---
title: On Keeping a Public Notebook
date: 2026-03-07
summary: Why publishing short technical notes is often more useful than waiting for a perfect long-form post.
---

I like the idea of a public notebook more than a formal blog.

A notebook gives me permission to publish:

- partial understanding
- short experiment summaries
- useful commands and references
- small decisions with clear tradeoffs

That style fits technical work better than waiting for a polished essay every time. It also leaves a trail I can reuse later when writing something more complete.
